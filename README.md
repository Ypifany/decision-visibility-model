# Decision Visibility Model

**You already decided. Can you see the decision?**

The Decision Visibility Model (DVM) is a practice from Cheryl Abram and Ypifany for making decisions visible. It does not grade a decision as right or wrong. It looks at the distance between what you expected and what reality is showing you, and treats that distance as information.

**[Try it](https://ypifany.github.io/decision-visibility-model/)**

## What you can try on this page

### The Clarity Check

Four short questions about one decision:

1. Which decision do you want to look at?
2. Who or what helped you decide?
3. Why did you choose it?
4. If it started to go off track, how would you know?

The result shows one of three clarity states:

| Clarity state | What it means |
|---|---|
| Functioning | You are tracking it and writing it down. If something shifts, you would see it in time to act. |
| Fragile | You are relying on a feeling or on someone else to tell you. You might see a shift, but maybe too late. |
| Blind | You have no way to know yet. You would find out when something goes wrong. |

### A short look back

Three questions: what you were hoping for, what you were betting on, and what happened. You sort each thing that happened into a kind of information, including "not sure, or it is a mix."

The page then shows up to four questions a Clarity Session would look at next, built from your answers. You can copy your notes to bring to a conversation with Cheryl, or print or save them as a PDF.

The Clarity Check and the look back change their wording for decisions that are behind you, still playing out, or not made yet.

The rest of the work, looking at what you really have and setting your next bet, happens in a Clarity Session and the three-session engagement with Cheryl.

## Privacy

The page runs entirely in the browser. It has no account, sends nothing, and saves nothing. Answers clear when the page is closed or reloaded. Please do not enter passwords or private information about other people.
